## What Together Mode is
Together Mode helps two people, each on their own phone, agree on a movie to watch. You create a family account, pick a "who's watching" profile, pair the two phones with a short code, and vote on movies. When you both say yes to the same movie, it's a match.

## Information we collect
- **Account information:** the email address and password used to create your family account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored securely — we never see or store your plain password.
- **Profiles:** the names you choose for each "who's watching" profile.
- **Your activity in the app:** which movies you vote yes or no on, your genre/taste preferences, and which movies you match on. This is used to personalize recommendations.
- **Basic usage events:** simple events such as "a session started" or "a match was found," used to understand how the app is used.

## What we do NOT collect
We do **not** access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. We do **not** use third-party advertising or ad-tracking.

## How we use your information
- To run the app — create accounts, pair the two phones, and save your profiles.
- To personalize movie recommendations to each profile's taste.
- To understand and improve how the app works.

## How information is stored and shared
Your data is stored with **Supabase**, a third-party hosting provider, on secured servers. Movie details are fetched from **The Movie Database (TMDB)**. We do **not** sell your data or share it with advertisers.
