## Decisions / to-dos for Dad before submitting

- **Age rating & category.** Because the app can show movie info up to R-rated *when a user picks the "Everyone / R" age option*, Apple's age-rating questionnaire will likely land it at **12+ or 17+** — answer the questionnaire honestly. Recommendation: publish as a **general Entertainment app, NOT in Apple's "Kids" category** (the Kids category forbids the R option and has the strictest data rules). This keeps things simpler and accurate.
- **Privacy Policy URL.** Apple requires a public link. Easiest options: paste the policy into a free host (e.g. a GitHub Pages page or a Google Doc set to "anyone with link"), or a simple site. The text is ready in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.
- **Screenshots** (required, captured from a phone/iPad): grab these four — the **home screen**, the **voting screen with a movie**, a **match screen**, and the **"Who's watching?" profiles**. Apple wants them at specific device sizes; EAS/App Store Connect will tell you which.
- **Bundle ID:** `com.asterkind.togethermode` (registered under the Asterkind company name).
